Privacy policy

Hermes Assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

  • It requests view-only access to the owner's Google Calendar and does not create, change or delete events.
  • Calendar data stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
